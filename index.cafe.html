About café in

A taste from Europe, made with love

café in is inspired by the warmth of European coffee houses — where the coffee is made with care, the pastries are fresh, and nobody is in a hurry.

Whether you're stopping by for a quick espresso, meeting friends for brunch or settling in with a book, our team is here to make every visit simple and enjoyable.

  • Artisan coffee, brewed fresh to order
  • Vegetarian-friendly menu with daily specials
  • Dine-in, takeaway and group bookings

Carefully sourced

Quality beans and fresh ingredients, prepared with attention to every detail.

Made fresh daily

Pastries, sandwiches and desserts prepared in-house so every plate arrives fresh.

Warm hospitality

Friendly service and a relaxed atmosphere — you're always welcome to stay a while.
